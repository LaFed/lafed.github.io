Privacy policy

LaFed is a personal assistant with a single user, its owner. No one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events, and adds or moves an event only after the owner confirms the change.
  • Gmail: reads messages and saves drafts. It has no way to send email.

Where the data goes

The sign-in key Google issues is kept in a file on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's account. Calendar and mail content is fetched when the owner asks a question and is passed to the AI services the assistant uses (Anthropic's Claude, and for speech ElevenLabs and OpenAI) only to answer that question. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

LaFed's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
